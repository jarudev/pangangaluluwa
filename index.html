Pangangaluluwa Songs for the Souls

In some places in the Philippines, people visit homes and sing special songs. They remember people who have died. Learn about Pangangaluluwa with ten picture questions.

A special Filipino tradition

Read each question and choose one of four pictures. You will see if your answer is right. Each correct answer lights one pumpkin.

Voice 1 Welcome! Pangangaluluwa is a tradition in some communities in the Philippines. People remember family and friends who have died.

Voice 2 It takes place around the end of October and the start of November. Groups walk from house to house. Some visitors wear white clothes or white sheets.

Voice 1 They act as souls of people who have died. Some people believe a soul is the part of a person that lives after death.

Voice 2 The visitors sing special songs outside homes. The songs ask families to say prayers for the souls. Prayers are words people say to God.

Voice 1 Families listen to the songs. They may give the visitors food or a little money. Sometimes they give rice cakes, a traditional Filipino food.

Voice 2 Families remember people who have died. This tradition brings people together. Some communities keep it so young people can learn about it. Are you ready? Let us play!
